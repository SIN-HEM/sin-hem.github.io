Privacy Policy

How user and device data is handled in the KhoKho mobile game and across bitsMAYA services.

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies directly to the mobile game KhoKho (the "Application"), developed and published by Hemant Singh operating under bitsMAYA ("we", "us", or "our"). This service is provided for use "AS IS".

We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency regarding how your information is handled when you install and play KhoKho.

2. Information Collection and Use

Direct Personal Data: We do not require you to create an account, register, or directly provide personally identifiable information (such as your real name, physical address, phone number, or email address) to play KhoKho. All local game progress, scores, and settings are stored locally on your device.

Automatically Collected Technical & Device Data

When you access and play the Application, certain technical data may be collected automatically by the game engine or integrated third-party services to ensure the game runs properly, fix bugs, and serve advertisements:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, language settings, and basic hardware identifiers.
  • Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) or similar resettable device identifiers used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.
  • Diagnostics & Performance Data: Crash logs, frame rate performance, launch times, and error telemetry.
  • Usage & Interaction Data: Basic session duration, game interaction events, and ad impression/click events.
  • Approximate Location: Non-precise location (such as country or region) inferred from your IP address for regional ad compliance and analytics.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

The Application is built using third-party development tools and may utilize third-party monetization and distribution services. These providers may collect and process information as governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Used for app distribution, core Android functionality, and security checks. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements within the Application. AdMob may use your device's Advertising ID and coarse location to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and prevent fraud. (AdMob & Google Partner Policies)
  • Unity Engine: The game engine powering KhoKho, which may collect basic hardware diagnostics and runtime telemetry to maintain stability across devices. (Unity Privacy Policy)
We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above to operate, maintain, and support the game.

4. User Controls & Opt-Out Options

You have full control over the data permissions and identifiers associated with your device:

  • Reset or Delete Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads at any time on your Android device by navigating to Settings > Google > All services > Ads.
  • Clear Local Data / Uninstall: You can erase all locally stored game data by clearing the Application's storage in your device settings, or stop all data collection completely by uninstalling the Application.

5. Data Retention & Security

Since bitsMAYA does not operate custom external servers to store your personal data, local gameplay data remains solely on your device until you clear the app cache or uninstall the game. Any diagnostic or advertising data collected by third-party partners (such as Google or Unity) is retained and secured in accordance with their respective data retention policies and industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS).

6. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly solicit or directly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Where advertisements are shown, ad serving configurations follow applicable platform policies. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has directly provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take steps to delete such information immediately.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in game features, legal requirements, or third-party integrations. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date" badge at the top.
